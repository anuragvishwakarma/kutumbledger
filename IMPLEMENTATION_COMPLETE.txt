All features have been implemented for the kutumbledger application:
- Transactions (CRUD with filtering, recurring)
- Accounts/Family Management (invitation via OTP, roles)
- Budgets (CRUD)
- Goals (CRUD with progress tracking)
- Money Jars (save/spend/give/invest with member assignment)
- Domestic Helpers (management with roles, salary, payment methods)
- Festival Plans (CRUD)
- Udhaar Records (lending/borrowing tracking)
